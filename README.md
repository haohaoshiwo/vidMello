### Zombie Love Story — AI Zombie Trend

[![Zombie Love Story on VidMello, with a generated sample frame of a couple embracing in a sunlit wheat field](https://vidmello.com/demo/zombie-couple-v1-og.jpg)](https://vidmello.com/templates/zombie-couple-video)

**Two portraits. One survivor. One partner who turns.**

VidMello's Zombie Couple Video Generator follows the emotional arc of the Zombie Love Story AI trend: a frightening encounter becomes recognition, a lowered weapon, an embrace, and a golden memory of life together.

Upload one clear portrait per adult and assign the roles:

- **The survivor:** the first person stays human, recognizes their partner, and opens their arms.
- **The one who turns:** the second person appears as a recognizable zombie in the cabin, then healthy in the warm memory.

The prepared story moves from a cold cabin encounter to a sunlit wheat-field reunion. It aims for a cinematic, non-graphic look without blood or gore. Either person can take either role; swap the photos to reverse the casting.

You receive an approximately 15-second, native 9:16 MP4 at the 720p setting, with the demo audio added. No custom prompt, filming, or scene assembly is required. Landscape output, custom story endings, and custom audio upload are not offered for this template.

## How it works

1. **Choose a template.** Watch its complete sample and check the photo requirements, format, and audio options.
2. **Add your photos.** Use clear, well-lit images with one visible subject per photo. Check the role labels when the template assigns different characters.
3. **Sign in and review the cost.** Each current template uses 10 Credits. Buy a one-time pack if needed, then explicitly submit your generation; payment alone does not start a video.
4. **Preview and download.** Once the job is accepted, it continues if you close the page. Return to private History to check progress and download the completed MP4.

## Privacy and creative expectations

Photos preview locally and upload only after sign-in and an explicit generation request. Input images are shared with the video provider to make your requested video. Uploads and results are private by default, with time-limited access links and media retention of up to 30 days. Download the files you want to keep.

AI can change faces, hands, gestures, and scene timing. Review the entire result before sharing. Audio is added after video generation; exact dialogue, sound-effect timing, and word-for-word lip sync are not guaranteed. Included audio does not grant a separate commercial music license or guarantee social-platform acceptance.

Use photos you own or have permission to upload and transform. Get consent before publicly sharing another person's romantic or zombie depiction.
